Scientific integration gates

Verify source snapshots and licenses; record cosmology, scale factor, coordinate frame, comoving/physical units, velocities, timestamps, stable halo IDs and uncertainties. Align constrained simulations only with validated anchors. Distinguish observed tracks, simulation histories, interpolations and future extrapolations.
