Evidence legend

DI direct imaging; RV stellar radial velocity; AST stellar or relative astrometry (see interpretation); SPEC spectroscopy; DD disk dynamics; ACC accretion emission. Spectroscopy, repeated relative astrometry, and disk signatures are not automatically independent confirmation techniques. No transits or microlensing are claimed for these entries.
