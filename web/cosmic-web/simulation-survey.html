Comparison protocol

  1. Ingest an identified simulation snapshot or lightcone, including cosmological parameters and units.
  2. Generate a synthetic observer lightcone and galaxies using an explicitly named model.
  3. Apply the same angular footprint, flux limit, redshift quality and completeness selection as the observed survey.
  4. Compare like-for-like statistics with uncertainties and cosmic variance.
  5. Keep observation, simulation, and interpretation independently inspectable.

No synthetic points are rendered here. This page does not imply a comparison has run.

Science case: GAMA's fading Universe

The 2015 GAMA panchromatic analysis measured the declining energy emitted by galaxies from ultraviolet through far-infrared wavelengths. ICRAR reported that the cosmic energy output measured in this study was about half that of two billion years earlier. This is not a measurement of the total energy of the Universe disappearing.

Comparison target: luminosity density by wavelength and redshift, with dust attenuation and star-formation histories in the forward model.
