Competing physical theories

Atlas evidence comparison · Research programmes are not equivalent, and mathematical consistency is not empirical confirmation.

Evidence policy: Each framework has a domain, a status, a limitation and sources. Chain Theory's proposed fine-structure constant identification remains conjectural. The Standard Model and General Relativity are tested frameworks; string theory and Chain Theory are not empirically confirmed replacements.

Interpretation

Cosmological models and fundamental unification programmes are not one-to-one competitors. Scientific comparison must be claim-specific: mathematical derivation, observed evidence, prediction, uncertainty, and unresolved assumptions should be tracked separately.
